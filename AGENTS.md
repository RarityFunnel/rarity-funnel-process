# Rarity Funnel archive maintenance

Read README.md, process/UPDATE_POLICY.md, catalog/coverage.json and provenance.json before changing this repository.

For every source or process change, update the corresponding originals or clearly labelled extracts, common catalog, door/section links, book–experience correspondences, provenance and change record together. Preserve origin IDs, versions, timestamps, owners and SHA-256 hashes. Preserve original source wording and distinguish decisions, proposals, implementation and tests.

Do not overwrite or promote approved manuscripts, scripts, canon, CURRENT, builds or permissions. Do not assume approval from a title or version number. Do not invent missing sources, routes, owners or completed synchronization. Never treat a URL as an archived copy of its target.

Publish only authorized public material. Keep credentials, visitor data, private correspondence and privately licensed audio out of the repository. Do not add a blanket license for third-party sources.

Before reporting a backup as complete, validate local relationships and hashes, then retrieve and verify the actual commit contents. Use non-forced updates with an expected branch head; inspect concurrent changes rather than overwriting them. Record real coverage and unresolved mismatches. Do not create empty commits.

This is an archive, not a website build. Do not enable deployments or add animations as part of archival maintenance. The policy does not itself create a background synchronization job.
