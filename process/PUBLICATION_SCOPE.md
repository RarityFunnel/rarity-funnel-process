# Publication scope

This first batch publishes source cards extracted from the experience data, the Books correspondence file, selected source-check apparatus, section views and archival records. Extracts are labelled and linked to their source ID, version and exact locator.

The full book manuscript, full approved script, full website source ZIP, private correspondence and licensed media are not included in this batch. References to those original objects describe provenance and are not claims that their complete bytes have been publicly backed up. External research papers have not been copied.

The project-level original files remain in their existing custody. No third-party licensing rights are inferred from a citation, a URL or inclusion in project documentation.
