# Source and process update policy

Owner mandate: David, 14 November 2026. Scope: the shared archive of Rarity Funnel sources and process from Books and Immersive Experience.

1. Each new source, correction, replacement, withdrawal, finding, decision, implementation change or test result must be included in the archive update for the corresponding delivery.
2. Locate existing originals first. Identify origin ID, version or modification timestamp, owner, scope and source locator. Do not request or duplicate already available material without a reason.
3. Keep one common catalog with door/section and book/experience correspondences. Deduplicate by source identity and content, not by title. Preserve conflicting versions and identify the responsible owner.
4. Preserve actual permitted original bytes or explicitly labelled extracts/exports. Record SHA-256 and extraction boundaries. A URL alone is not a backup of a document.
5. Keep decisions, proposals, implementation records and tests distinct. Archived pending checks are not proof of approval. Never infer approval from a newer title or delivery counter.
6. Update the affected originals, catalog, section pages, provenance, coverage and process record together in a versioned commit. Preserve history and mark withdrawals without deleting their provenance.
7. Retrieve the committed files by immutable commit ID, compare content and hashes, and check the branch head before marking the change backed up. A prepared file, deposit or commit without readback does not prove completed synchronization.
8. Books and Immersive Experience provide their existing indexes and subsequent deltas through verified project routes. RF maintains the shared replica; it does not change approved manuscripts, scripts, canon, permissions or builds. Navigation/Home remains with its competent owner.
9. Publish only material authorized for this public archive. Restricted originals require an authorized private destination. Do not publish credentials, visitor data, private correspondence or privately licensed audio.
10. No empty updates or repeated pending reports. Automatic checking is a separate operational capability and must not be claimed until configured and verified.

This policy is installed in this repository. Adoption by other project workflows requires their actual return; it is not inferred from a copy sent to them. GitHub is a replica and does not replace project preservation governance.
